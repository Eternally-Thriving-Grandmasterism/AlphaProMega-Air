## Objective

Produce a **bounded study pack** that answers, for future airframe concepts:

1. Predicted **Δeffective dose** (polar vs equatorial, FL350–400 class) for candidate material stacks  
2. **Mass penalty** vs aluminum/CFRP baseline  
3. What **fails first** (fire, fatigue, inspectability, cost)  
4. Confirmation that **Tier A operational ALARA still required**
